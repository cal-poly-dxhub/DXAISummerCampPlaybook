# Cal State AI Builders

## Community Management Phases

### 1. Pre-Event: Community Interactions and Onboarding

- **welcome**: Serves as the initial touchpoint for new members, providing introductions and basic information.
- **faq**: Houses frequently asked questions to help newcomers get up to speed quickly.
- **mentors**: A space for mentors to introduce themselves, projects they've worked on with github links, 'hello world' repo's and tutorials showing core AI/Cloud concepts, and for participants to seek guidance.
- **social**: Encourages informal interactions to build community spirit before the event.

### 2. During the Event: Managing Active Projects and Collaboration

- **Project-specific channels** (e.g., 10-training, 11-shipping, etc.): Dedicated spaces for each team to collaborate, share updates, and seek help.
- **all-cal-state-ai-builders** / **general**: Main channel for event-wide announcements and discussions.
- **camp-staff**: Private channel for organizers to coordinate and manage the event.
- **mentor-debate**: Private channel for mentors to discuss challenges and strategies.
- **Huddles** / **Server Boosts**: Features to enhance real-time communication and server performance during the event.

### 3. Post-Event: Long-term Community Engagement and Expansion

- **Project channels**: Remain active for ongoing development and updates on projects.
- **social**: Continues to foster community connections and informal discussions.
- **mentors**: Provides ongoing support and guidance for community members.
- **all-cal-state-ai-builders** / **general**: Shares updates on project progress, new opportunities, and community news.
- **faq**: Updated with new information relevant to the growing community.
- **Drafts & sent**: (If applicable) Used for planning and coordinating future events or initiatives.

The structure of these environments allows for seamless transition between phases, supporting community growth from initial onboarding through active collaboration and into long-term engagement. By maintaining active channels and encouraging ongoing interaction, the community can expand its reach, keep projects alive, and foster continued connectivity among members.
