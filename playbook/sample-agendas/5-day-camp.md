## Camp Themes

### Core Values

- **Builder Mentality:** Hands-on problem-solving and creating solutions
- **Culture of Innovation in the Age of AI:** Embracing change and emerging technologies
- **Building Durable Skills:** Developing foundational capabilities that remain valuable as technology evolves
- **Leveraging AI to Augment Skills:** Using AI tools to enhance and amplify human capabilities

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of the camp, participants will:

- Understand core AI concepts and large language models
- Have hands-on experience with AI development tools
- Complete a real-world project with team collaboration
- Present their solution to industry experts
- Build a network of peers and mentors in AI
- Receive a certificate of completion
- Have a GitHub repository showcasing their work

### Participant Demographics

- **Academic Focus:** Community College system students across various programs
- **Experience Level:** Mixed undergraduate and graduate students
- **Institution:** 2-year degree system within California Community College System
- **Skills Range:** Beginner to intermediate technical backgrounds
