# Launching the Next Generation of AI Innovators

Are you ready to shape the future of artificial intelligence? Join us for an intensive week of hands-on AI development at the beautiful Cal Poly San Luis Obispo campus! Students will work on cross collaborative teams to solve pressing challenges and opportunities in the California Community College system. Problems will be documented in advance of the camp and student teams will learn core AI skillsets and apply them to those problems throughout the week. At the end of the week, teams will pitch their ideas and prototypes to their peers and mentors for prizes. It will be an unforgettable summer camp experience.

---

**Those that are selected for the scholarship will receive lodging and 3 meals per day at no cost. All majors and backgrounds welcome!**

## What You'll Learn

Apply cutting-edge AI technologies through hands-on workshops and mentored projects:

- Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multi-Modal AI Systems
- Vector Databases and Embedding Technologies
- Advanced Prompt Engineering
- Full Stack AI Application Development
- Real-World AI Implementation Strategies
- Agentic AI concepts
- Team work
- Applied problem solving
- Presentation skills

## Who Should Apply

- All majors welcome - no prior AI experience required
- Passionate about technology and innovation
- Ready to tackle real-world challenges with AI
