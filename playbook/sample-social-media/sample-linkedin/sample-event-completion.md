Out of nearly 900 applicants, only 100 students from across the CSU system were selected for the CSU AI Summer Camp 2025, hosted by the Cal Poly Digital Transformation Hub and powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was fortunate to be one of them — and the week at the beautiful Cal Poly San Luis Obispo campus was an experience I’ll never forget.

The camp was packed with hands-on labs, from LLMs to RAG applications to advanced prompt engineering and more — each packed with practical knowledge we could apply right away. Throughout the week, every camper worked on real-world challenges submitted by CSU campuses. It was inspiring to see teams tackling problems that genuinely affect students, faculty, and staff — and knowing that what we were building could have an impact far beyond the camp itself.

Out of 15 teams, our team, 𝗗𝗲𝗹𝘁𝗮𝟲𝟭 — Colin Ngo, Elias Santillan, Marisabel Perez Moreno, Dianella Sy, and I — worked on a challenge from California Polytechnic State University-San Luis Obispo: handling the 750+ repetitive math placement questions a single staff member receives each year. Our solution, 𝗠𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗣𝗮𝘁𝗵 𝗔𝗜, is an AI-powered chatbot that authenticates students, uses their academic record for personalized guidance, and references verified university policy through AWS Bedrock to provide accurate, real-time answers. We were awarded 𝟯𝗿𝗱 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗰𝗲 in the hackathon.

The week wasn’t all work — we also had unique, tech-powered activities that made learning even more exciting. One of my favorites was the AI-powered scavenger hunt, where we followed NFC-tagged rubber ducks that led us to an AI chatbot for hints about the next duck’s location. It was not only fun to play but also fascinating to see the creative technology that brought the game to life. Huge thanks to the team that made this happen!

What stood out most wasn’t just the technology we learned and built — it was the community we built. I’m grateful to have been part of it.
