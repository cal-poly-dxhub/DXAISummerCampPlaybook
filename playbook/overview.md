# Playbook Overview

## Event Goal & Framework

**Mission:** Create applied AI learning experiences that connect learners with real-world challenges while producing open-source solutions for organizational problems.

**Core Concept:** Educational institutions and enterprise departments benefit from a structured framework that transforms authentic organizational challenges into hands-on learning opportunities for students and staff.

## Value Proposition

### Challenge Sponsors (Public/Private Sector) Receive:

1. **Problem identification and scoping** at no cost
2. **Multiple prototype solutions** with minimal resource investment
3. **Access to innovative approaches** from diverse teams
4. **Open-source deliverables** for future development

### Participants (Students/Staff) Receive:

1. **Applied AI skill development** through real problem-solving
2. **Authentic challenge experience** with measurable impact
3. **Interdisciplinary teamwork** and presentation opportunities
4. **Certificate of completion** and professional recognition
5. **GitHub repository** showcasing their contributions

## Flexible Duration Framework

### 1-Day Workshops

- Rapid problem exploration and ideation
- Introduction to AI concepts
- Basic prototype development
- Team formation and networking

### 3-Day Hackathons

- Intensive prototype development
- Focused skill building sessions
- Mentor-guided project work
- Competitive presentation format

### 5-Day Camps

- Comprehensive learning with multiple skill tracks
- Deep dive into AI technologies
- Extensive team building and collaboration
- Guest speakers and industry insights

### 12-Week Courses/Events (virtual or in classroom)

- Deep curriculum integration with ongoing projects
- Progressive skill development
- Multiple challenge cycles
- Sustained mentorship and community building

## Educational Integration

This framework is highly valuable for educational systems and large enterprise departments as it creates structured, outcome-driven AI learning experiences that address real organizational needs while developing participant capabilities.

### Key Success Factors:

- **Real-world relevance** - challenges come from actual organizational needs
- **Hands-on learning** - participants build actual solutions, not just theoretical knowledge
- **Community building** - sustained engagement through collaborative platforms
- **Open source outcomes** - solutions benefit the broader community
- **Scalable framework** - adaptable to different time commitments and skill levels
