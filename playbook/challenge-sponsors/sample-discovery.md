# 30-Minute Virtual Discovery Interview Guide

​

## Setup

​

- Schedule with problem submitter/expert using email template customized to submitter
- Prepare for note-taking or recording (preferred)
- Ideally full time DX Hub staff, tenured student developer, and discovery specialist role attend
  ​

## Opening (3 min)

​
**Intro's/Background:** "Intent of CSU Summer Camp"
**Recording:** "Can I record this for internal notes only?"
**Purpose:** "I need to understand your problem, workflow, and data needs to build the right solution."
​

## Discovery Questions (22 min)

​

### Current Workflow

​

- Walk through your process step-by-step
- Who's involved and what are their roles?
- Where are the bottlenecks and pain points?
  ​

### Impact & Goals

​

- How does this problem affect your team/organization?
- What would perfect success look like? Desribe the new experience.
  ​

### Success Metrics

​

- How would you measure success?
- What KPI's/metrics?
  ​

### Data Requirements _(Critical)_

​

- What data do you need to solve this?
- Where is it stored (systems, format)?
- Can you provide samples, screenshots, or documentation?
- If not, can you describe data structure/schema?
- Any access restrictions?
  ​

## Action Items (3 min)

​
**Data deliverables needed:**
​

- Sample data/files or detailed schemas
- Web links to data or key resources
- Screenshots of current systems (if applicable)
- Process documentation (if available/applicable)
- Timeline: [set specific date e.g. emailed by this Friday]
  ​

## Closing (2 min)

​

- Schedule 30-min follow-up session (if needed)
- Connect us with other key stakeholders
- Confirm next steps and timeline
- Invite the option to mentor virtually or in person during the camp week (optional)
- Invite to the camp presentations on last day (Friday)
  ​

## Success Check

​
You should leave with:
​

- Clear workflow and pain point understanding and documentation
- Defined success metrics
- Committed data delivery timeline
- Additional stakeholder contacts (if applicable)
- Follow-up scheduled (if applicable)
