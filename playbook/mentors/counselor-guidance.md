### Counselors are different than mentors in that they are external visitors/stakeholders from organizations with a vested interest in camp outcomes.

# AI Summer Camp Counselor Guidelines - KBYG

## Key Role & Responsibilities

- Primary role: Observer and learner first
- Secondary role: Subject matter expert/advisor when relevant
- Third role: Team encouragement and resource connection

## Project Interaction Guidelines

- Provide subject matter expertise when asked, but DO NOT direct team development work
- Maintain balanced time distribution across multiple teams
- Teams should operate independently of counselor presence
- Help teams find resources and connect with relevant mentors
- Encourage problem-solving and team progress

## Participation Framework

- Welcome to participate in:
  - Workshops
  - Hands-on labs
  - Team building activities
  - Social events
  - Meals (same access as campers)

## Attendance

- Flexible attendance - not required all 5 days
- No mandatory session attendance
- Check-ins are optional

## Support Structure

- 15 core team mentors (AI experts) will be present
- Multiple mentorship sources available to campers

## Logistics

### Lodging

- Counselors responsible for own accommodations
- Recommended Hotels:
  - Courtyard San Luis Obispo
  - Springhill Suites by Marriott San Luis Obispo
  - The Wayfarer San Luis Obispo, Tapestry Collection by Hilton
- Student Housing: Buena Vista building in Poly Canyon Village

### Parking

- Counselors and Guests: [Grand Avenue Parking Structure](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XBkcmUySDVvo9gcx8)
- Parking cost will be covered

### Meals

- Breakfast, lunch, and dinner provided
- Dinner optional if counselors prefer off-site dining

## Important Notes

- Press may be present during the event
- Teams should develop self-sufficiency
- Focus on fostering learning environment rather than directing outcomes

## Best Practices

- Maintain balanced engagement across teams
- Share expertise when relevant without taking over
- Support team independence
- Participate in community aspects of camp experience
- Act as cheerleader and connector for teams
