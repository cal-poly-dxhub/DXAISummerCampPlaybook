## Example Submission: Streamlining Scholarships

**Status:** Submitted  
**Date Submitted:** 7/21/2025 7:34 PM  
**Name of Community College:** [Community College District Name]  
**Name of Challenge Sponsor:** [Sponsor Name]  
**Title of Challenge Sponsor:** Assistant Director, Foundation  
**Challenge Sponsor CCC Email Address:** [email@college.edu]  
**Project Name:** Streamlining Scholarships: Smarter Matching, Faster Awards, Greater Access  
**Is Sample (or Synthetic) Data Available for This Project?** Yes

### Problem or Opportunity

Our college foundation manages dozens of scholarships, funded by various donors, each with unique eligibility requirements, application processes, and deadlines. While the financial support is critical for students, the administrative process is burdensome and student participation is too low.

Key issues include:

- Students don't always hear about scholarships or miss deadlines
- Applications are time-consuming, repetitive, or unclear
- Staff must manually review, score, and match applicants across many funding sources
- Award notifications and fund disbursement are often delayed due to manual workflows
- There's limited capacity to track equity gaps in who applies and receives funding

### Customers/Stakeholders

Foundation staff, reviewers, donors, student scholarship applicants and recipients

### Value Proposition

- Helps students complete their applications (maybe with one simplified application that matches to eligible scholarships)
- Ease of reading/matching donor criteria and align applicants to best-fit funds
- Sends personalized nudges or reminders to students based on academic standing, financial aid status, or interests
- Streamlines reviewer scoring to help reviewers or somehow helps us get more reviewers and makes it a less arduous process and more enjoyable. Is it possible to gamify it?
- Speeds up award and disbursement tracking with a dashboard for foundation staff
- Surfaces equity analytics (e.g., who's applying vs. who's awarded)

### Success Metrics

- Less time for staff to advertise, review, disburse funds
- More awards for students
- Every scholarship has applicants and is competitive to some extent
- Ease of application and funding match
- Equity analytics (applications vs awards)

### Current Technology Environment

Academic Works Scholarship portal, Banner administrative software suite – by Ellucian

### Available Resources

Unknown at this time. Depending on solution and proposed cost, we could look at possible donor funding or deploy funding from Academic Works towards this project instead if it were to replace that portal system.
